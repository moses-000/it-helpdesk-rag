# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

## What MFA is
Multi-factor authentication means signing in with your password plus a second proof, normally an approval in the Microsoft Authenticator app. MFA is mandatory for all Harrowgate accounts when signing in to Microsoft 365, the VPN and the HR system from outside the office network.

## Setting up MFA
Install Microsoft Authenticator from the App Store or Google Play on your phone. On a computer, go to https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo, sign in, select "Add sign-in method", choose Authenticator app and scan the QR code with the app. Add a phone number as a backup method in case you lose access to the app.

## Getting a new phone
Before you reset or trade in your old phone, add the Authenticator app to your new phone using the security info page while you still have the old one. If you have already lost the old phone, call the Service Desk on extension 4400; after verifying your identity they can issue a Temporary Access Pass that lasts 8 hours so you can register the new device.

## Unexpected MFA prompts
If you receive an MFA approval request that you did not start, always press Deny and report it immediately to security@harrowgate.example. An unexpected prompt means someone may know your password, so change your password straight away. Never approve a prompt just to make it go away – this is known as an MFA fatigue attack.
