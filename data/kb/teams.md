# Microsoft Teams Problems

## Teams will not open or shows a blank screen
Fully quit Teams by right-clicking its icon in the system tray and choosing Quit. Then clear the Teams cache: press Windows+R, type %localappdata%\Packages\MSTeams_8wekyb3d8bbwe\LocalCache\Microsoft\MSTeams and press Enter, delete everything in that folder, and restart Teams. Clearing the cache does not delete any chats or files, which are stored in the cloud.

## Microphone or camera not working in meetings
In Teams, go to Settings > Devices and check the correct microphone, speaker and camera are selected. Check Windows privacy settings: Settings > Privacy & security > Camera and Microphone must both allow desktop apps. If your laptop has a physical camera shutter or a mute key with a light, make sure they are not active. Close other apps such as Zoom that may be holding the camera.

## Missing Teams or channels
If you cannot see a team you expect, ask the team owner to add you – IT does not manage membership of individual teams. New teams are created through the "Request a Team" form on the intranet and must have at least two owners.

## External guests
External guests can be invited to teams and meetings. Guests have access only to the team they are invited to and are removed automatically after 90 days of inactivity.
