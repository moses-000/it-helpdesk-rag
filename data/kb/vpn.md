# Connecting to the VPN

## When you need the VPN
The GlobalProtect VPN is needed to reach internal systems from outside the office, including the finance system, shared network drives (the S: and H: drives) and the intranet. Microsoft 365 services such as Outlook, Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint work without the VPN.

## How to connect
Click the GlobalProtect globe icon in the system tray (bottom right of the taskbar), make sure the portal address is vpn.harrowgate.example, and click Connect. Approve the MFA prompt on your phone. When connected, the icon shows a shield and the status reads "Connected".

## VPN will not connect
First confirm your internet connection works by opening any public website. If you are on hotel or public Wi-Fi, open a browser first and accept the Wi-Fi terms page, because captive portals block the VPN. Next, right-click the GlobalProtect icon, choose Refresh Connection, and try again. If you see "Gateway not responding", restart your laptop. If you see an authentication error, your password may have expired – see the password reset article.

## Slow connection on VPN
Video calls should be made in Teams directly and do not need the VPN. If file access is slow, save working copies to OneDrive rather than opening large files over the S: drive. Home connections below 10 Mbps upload will feel slow when working on shared drives.
