# Reporting Phishing and Suspicious Emails

## Signs of a phishing email
Be suspicious of emails that create urgency, ask you to sign in through a link, request payment or bank detail changes, or come from a sender whose display name does not match the email address. Emails from outside the company show an orange "EXTERNAL" banner at the top. Hover over links before clicking to see the real destination.

## How to report
In Outlook, select the email and click the "Report" button on the Home ribbon, then choose "Report phishing". This sends the email to the security team and removes it from your inbox. Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues to ask their opinion.

## If you clicked a link or opened an attachment
Do not panic and do not try to hide it. Disconnect the laptop from the network (turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the cable), then immediately call the IT Service Desk on extension 4400 and say it is a security incident. If you entered your password on a suspicious page, change your password straight away from another device. Reporting quickly is what matters – nobody is disciplined for reporting a genuine mistake.

## Payment and bank detail requests
Any request to change supplier bank details must be verified by phoning the supplier on a number from our own records, never a number contained in the email.
