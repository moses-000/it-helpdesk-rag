# New Starter IT Setup

## Before the first day
Line managers must submit a New Starter request in the IT portal at least 5 working days before the start date. The request should include the job title, department, start date, any shared mailboxes or drives needed, and whether the person needs a laptop, a mobile phone or both.

## On the first day
The new starter collects their laptop from the IT desk on the ground floor between 9:00 and 10:00. IT gives them a temporary password, and they must set up MFA and change their password before leaving the IT desk. The first sign-in takes about 20 minutes while the laptop installs apps and syncs settings.

## Mandatory training
Every new starter must complete the Cyber Security Awareness e-learning module within their first 7 days. Accounts are flagged to the line manager if the training is not completed.

## Leavers
When someone leaves, the line manager must submit a Leaver request. The account is disabled at 17:00 on the leaving date, and the laptop, phone, ID badge and any accessories must be returned to IT on or before the last day. The leaver's mailbox is kept for 90 days and can be shared with the manager on request.
