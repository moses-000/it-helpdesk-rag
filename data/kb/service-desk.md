# Contacting the IT Service Desk

## Ways to get help
The IT portal at https://itportal.harrowgate.example is the fastest way to raise a ticket for non-urgent issues and requests. For urgent issues, call extension 4400 (or +44 20 7946 0000 from outside the office). You can also visit the IT desk on the ground floor of the London office.

## Opening hours
The Service Desk is staffed Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UK time. Outside these hours, calls are answered by an on-call engineer for urgent issues only: lost or stolen devices, security incidents, and outages affecting a whole team.

## Priorities and response times
Tickets are prioritised as follows. Priority 1 (critical, many users affected or a security incident): response within 30 minutes. Priority 2 (one user cannot work): response within 4 working hours. Priority 3 (issue with a workaround): response within 1 working day. Priority 4 (requests and questions): response within 3 working days.

## What to include in a ticket
Include your laptop's asset tag, a description of what you were trying to do, the exact error message, and a screenshot if possible. Tickets with screenshots are typically resolved much faster.
