# Lost or Stolen Laptop or Phone

## Report immediately
If a company laptop or phone is lost or stolen, call the IT Service Desk on extension 4400 immediately, or +44 20 7946 0000 from outside the office. The Service Desk runs 24/7 for lost device reports. Report it even if you think you might find it later.

## What IT will do
IT will lock the device remotely, sign it out of all company apps, and reset your password. Company phones are remotely wiped; laptops are protected by BitLocker encryption and will be wiped when next connected to the internet.

## Police report
For theft, report it to the police and send the crime reference number to IT. This is required for the insurance claim.

## Personal phones with company apps
If you use a personal phone with Outlook or Teams, IT can remove company data from those apps only, without touching your personal photos or data.

## Replacement
A replacement laptop is normally available within 2 working days. Your files will be restored automatically from OneDrive.
