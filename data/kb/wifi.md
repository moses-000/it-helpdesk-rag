# Office Wi-Fi

## Networks available
There are three Wi-Fi networks in Harrowgate offices. HG-Corporate is for company laptops and connects automatically using your device certificate – you should never need to enter a password. HG-Guest is for visitors and personal devices; it is internet-only and needs a daily code from reception. HG-IoT is reserved for printers and meeting-room equipment and must not be used by staff.

## Company laptop will not join HG-Corporate
Make sure Wi-Fi is switched on and airplane mode is off. Select HG-Corporate, click Forget, then reconnect. If your laptop has not connected to the office network or VPN for more than 60 days, its certificate may have expired: connect using a network cable at any desk, leave the laptop signed in for 15 minutes so the certificate renews, then try Wi-Fi again.

## Personal phones
Personal phones should use HG-Guest. Do not install company certificates on personal devices.
