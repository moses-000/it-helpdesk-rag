# Account Locked Out

## Why accounts get locked
Your account is locked automatically after 5 failed sign-in attempts within 15 minutes. This protects against password-guessing attacks. The most common cause is an old password saved on a phone, tablet or mapped drive that keeps trying to sign in in the background after you change your password.

## Unlocking your account
Accounts unlock automatically after 30 minutes. If you cannot wait, use the Self-Service Password Reset portal and choose "Unlock my account" – you do not need to change your password to unlock. Alternatively call the IT Service Desk on extension 4400.

## Repeated lockouts
If your account keeps locking, check every device where your work account is signed in. Remove and re-add the account on your phone's mail app, disconnect and reconnect any mapped network drives, and sign out of Teams on any old devices. If the problem continues, raise a ticket so IT can check the domain controller logs and identify which device is sending the bad password.
