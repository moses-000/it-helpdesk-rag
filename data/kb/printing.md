# Printing and Secure Print

## Secure print (Follow-Me printing)
All office printers use secure print. When you print, choose the printer called "HG-SecurePrint". Your job is held on the print server for 24 hours and released when you tap your ID badge on any office printer. This means you can print at your desk and collect the pages from any floor.

## Adding the printer
HG-SecurePrint is installed automatically on company laptops. If it is missing, open Company Portal, search for "Secure Print", and click Install. You must be in the office or on the VPN for the install to work.

## Printer shows my job but will not release it
Check that your ID badge is registered for printing: the first time you use a printer, tap your badge and enter your username and password on the printer's screen to link the badge. If the printer says "Badge not recognised" after linking, raise a ticket – your badge may need re-encoding by Facilities.

## Paper jams and toner
For paper jams, follow the animation on the printer's screen and open only the doors it shows. Toner replacement is handled by the facilities team; report low toner through the Facilities request form, not the IT Service Desk.
