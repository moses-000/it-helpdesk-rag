# OneDrive and File Storage

## Where to save files
Save personal working files in OneDrive, which gives each user 1 TB of storage backed up in the cloud. Save files your team needs to share in the relevant Teams channel or SharePoint site. Do not save work files only on your laptop's C: drive or desktop outside OneDrive, because they are not backed up and will be lost if the laptop fails.

## Sync problems
A red X or a sync-pending icon on the OneDrive cloud in the taskbar means files are not syncing. Click the icon to see the error. Common causes are file names containing characters such as " * : < > ? / \ |, file paths longer than 400 characters, and files open in another program. Rename or close the file and sync will resume. If sync is stuck completely, click the OneDrive icon, choose Settings > Pause syncing, wait a minute and resume.

## Restoring deleted files
Files deleted from OneDrive go to the OneDrive recycle bin at onedrive.com, where they are kept for 93 days. To restore a whole OneDrive to an earlier point, for example after ransomware or accidental mass deletion, go to OneDrive settings online and choose "Restore your OneDrive", which can roll back up to 30 days.

## Legacy network drives
The H: (home) drive is read-only and is being retired; copy anything you still need into OneDrive. The S: shared drive is still in use for finance and engineering drawings and requires the VPN when working remotely.
