# Resetting Your Password

Applies to: all staff with a Harrowgate network account.

## Self-service password reset
You can reset your own password at any time using the Self-Service Password Reset (SSPR) portal at https://passwordreset.harrowgate.example. You must have registered a mobile number or authenticator app first. Select "Forgot my password", enter your work email, complete the verification step, and choose a new password. The new password works on your laptop, email and Teams within 5 minutes.

## Password rules
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and cannot contain your name or username. You cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords. Passphrases made of several unrelated words (for example "copper-lantern-river-9") are recommended because they are long but easy to remember. Passwords expire every 180 days, and you will get an email reminder 14 days before expiry.

## If self-service does not work
If you have not registered for SSPR, or the portal rejects your verification, call the IT Service Desk on extension 4400. For security reasons the Service Desk will verify your identity using your employee number and a code sent to your line manager before resetting your password. The Service Desk will never ask you for your current password.

## After resetting
After a reset, update the saved password on your mobile phone's email app and on any shared printers where you use secure print. If you are working remotely, connect to the VPN first so that your laptop picks up the new password; otherwise you may need to use your old password to log in to Windows until you next connect.
