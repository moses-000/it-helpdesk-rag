# BitLocker Recovery Key

## Why you are seeing the BitLocker screen
All company laptops are encrypted with BitLocker to protect data if a laptop is lost or stolen. Occasionally, after a BIOS update, a hardware change or several failed PIN attempts, the laptop starts on a blue screen asking for a 48-digit BitLocker recovery key.

## Getting your recovery key
On another device such as your phone, go to https://myaccount.microsoft.com, sign in with your work account, open Devices, select your laptop and choose "View BitLocker Keys". Match the Key ID shown on the blue screen with the one on the website, then type the 48-digit recovery key. If you cannot access the website, call the Service Desk on extension 4400; they will read out the key after verifying your identity.

## After recovery
Once Windows starts, restart the laptop once more to check it boots normally. If the recovery screen appears repeatedly, raise a ticket because the laptop may need its encryption settings repaired.
