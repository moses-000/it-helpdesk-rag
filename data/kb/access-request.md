# Requesting Access to Systems and Folders

## How to request access
Access to shared drives, SharePoint sites, shared mailboxes and business applications is requested through the IT portal under "Access requests". Choose the system, state the level of access (read or edit) and give the business reason. Requests are sent automatically to the owner of the resource for approval.

## Approval times
Once the owner approves, access is granted within 1 working day. If the owner has not approved within 3 working days, the request is escalated to your line manager. IT cannot grant access to a folder or site without the owner's approval, even if your manager asks.

## Finance and HR systems
Access to the finance and HR systems also requires completion of the relevant system training and approval from the head of that department.

## Access reviews
Resource owners review who has access every 6 months. Access that is no longer needed is removed, so if you lose access after moving teams, submit a new request.
