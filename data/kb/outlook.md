# Outlook and Email

## Mailbox full
Each mailbox has 50 GB of storage and an unlimited online archive. When you reach 49 GB you will receive a warning, and at 50 GB you can no longer send email. Empty the Deleted Items folder and move old mail to the Online Archive folder in Outlook. Emails older than two years are moved to the archive automatically.

## Outlook is not receiving new email
Check the status bar at the bottom of Outlook: "Working offline" means offline mode is on – go to the Send/Receive tab and click Work Offline to turn it off. If it says "Disconnected", check your internet connection. You can also check Outlook on the web at https://outlook.office.com; if email arrives there but not in the desktop app, close Outlook and restart it.

## Shared mailboxes
Access to a shared mailbox (such as accounts@ or hr@) must be approved by the mailbox owner. Request it through the IT portal under "Access requests". Once granted, the shared mailbox appears in Outlook automatically within about 60 minutes.

## Out of office
Set an automatic reply in Outlook via File > Automatic Replies. Set separate messages for internal and external senders, and avoid including your travel dates or home details in external replies.
